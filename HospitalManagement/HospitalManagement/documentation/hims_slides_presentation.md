## Slide 3: Feasibility & Viability
Technical and operational breakdowns illustrating why the HIMS solution is both achievable and sustainable.

![Slide 3 - Feasibility & Viability](file:///C:/Users/vikne/.gemini/antigravity/brain/7077f244-5521-4c29-99be-5f14c6bf8219/slide3_feasibility.png)

### Feasibility:
The **Hospital Information Management System (HIMS)** is technically and operationally feasible as it is developed using reliable and widely adopted technologies such as **React.js, ASP.NET Core Web API, C#, Entity Framework Core, MySQL, JWT Authentication, Biometric Face & Fingerprint Verification, and RESTful APIs**. The system can be deployed using standard hardware and software infrastructure, requires minimal maintenance, and provides a secure, HIPAA-compliant, and modular architecture suitable for modern healthcare organizations.

### Viability:
The **Hospital Information Management System (HIMS)** is a viable solution because it streamlines clinical and administrative lifecycles by reducing patient registration time, preventing duplicate medical records through intelligent deduplication, ensuring secure access via dual-factor biometrics, and facilitating automated pharmacy and billing processes. These features enhance patient care quality and provider efficiency while reducing administrative operational costs, making the system highly practical, sustainable, and beneficial for long-term clinical use.
